# Code of conduct

This is a support space for people who trade with POLYDESK. Keep it useful:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Help others the way you would want to be helped: concrete steps, version numbers,
  what you saw and what you expected.
- **Never share private keys, API tokens or wallet addresses** — yours or anyone
  else's. Posts containing them are removed immediately.
- No financial advice, market tips, referral or promotional content, or harassment.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and block accounts that ignore this.
Concerns: admin@itereon.eu.
